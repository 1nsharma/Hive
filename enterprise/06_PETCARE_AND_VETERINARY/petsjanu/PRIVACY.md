# 📜 PetJanu Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Website:** [https://petsjanu.com](https://petsjanu.com)  
**Contact:** `privacy@petsjanu.com` / `support@petsjanu.com`

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided by Pet Parents
- **Account & Contact Data:** Name, mobile number (+91), email address, city/locality.
- **Pet Profiles:** Pet name, species, breed, age, photo, vaccination history, medical alerts, and dietary requirements.
- **Order & Payment Data:** Billing address and Razorpay transaction references (we do NOT store full credit/debit card numbers or CVV).

### B. Information Collected During Public Tag Scans
- **Finder Sightings:** When a finder scans a QR/NFC tag or reports a sighting, optional GPS coordinates, uploaded photo, and notes are transmitted to notify the pet parent.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Lost Pet Reunions:** Enabling finders to alert pet parents via masked relay calls and instant WhatsApp notifications.
- **AI Health Assistance:** Contextualizing symptom queries with your pet's species and breed profile to generate accurate triage advice.
- **Service Fulfillment:** Processing marketplace orders and vet appointments.
- **Vaccination Reminders:** Sending timely SMS/WhatsApp alerts for annual pet boosters.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Phone Masking

- **No Public Number Harvesting:** Public QR tag profiles mask your primary phone number to protect pet parents from spam or harassment.
- **No Data Selling:** PetJanu never sells, rents, or monetizes user personal data to third-party ad networks.

---

## 4. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

- **Access & Correction:** You can view and update your account details at any time from the **Account Settings** page.
- **Data Deletion:** You can request complete account and pet profile deletion by emailing `support@petsjanu.com`.
- **Notification Preferences:** Toggle WhatsApp and SMS alert preferences directly in your Account Settings.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

PetJanu utilizes trusted sub-processors for infrastructure:
- **Cloud Hosting & Database:** Google Cloud Platform / Firebase / Supabase
- **Payments:** Razorpay (RBI Licensed Payment Gateway)
- **AI Care Models:** Google AI Studio (Gemini 3.6 Flash)
