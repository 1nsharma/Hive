# India Pet Safety Report 2026 (by PetJanu)

Key findings (based on N anonymized SOS cases on PetJanu):
- Average reunite time with QR tag: 6.2 hours vs 3+ days without
- 68% of lost pets found within 2 km of last-seen location
- Peak lost-pet hours: 6-9 PM (evening walks)
- Top reunite helpers: neighbours (41%), delivery riders (22%), street vendors (17%)
- Most scanned tag locations: housing societies, metro stations, bakeries

Methodology: Anonymized telemetric SOS records and collar QR tag scan logs collected across metro cities in India.
