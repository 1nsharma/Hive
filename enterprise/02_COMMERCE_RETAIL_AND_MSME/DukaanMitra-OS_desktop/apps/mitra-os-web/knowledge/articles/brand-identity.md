## Detailed Description
DukaanMitra is an Indian retail technology platform focused on digitising kirana stores and small neighbourhood retailers through a WhatsApp-first operating model. Its core value proposition is to help shop owners replace manual bahi-khata processes, fragmented inventory tracking, and informal udhaar management with an AI-assisted digital workflow that works inside WhatsApp, without requiring a separate app download or complex ERP-style onboarding. 

The platform is positioned as an AI-powered "Digital Munim" for Bharat retailers, enabling merchants to record transactions, monitor sales, track inventory, and manage customer credit in a format that matches existing shopkeeper behaviour and messaging habits.

The company's product experience centres on Munim AI, which uses natural language processing to interpret transaction messages such as customer name, amount, and item details from free-form WhatsApp text. DukaanMitra then converts those inputs into structured digital records, updates ledgers in real time, and generates outputs such as customer statements, end-of-day reports, stock alerts, and retail intelligence.

Its technology stack and workflow references include WhatsApp API, NLP, Gemini, BERT, Firebase, Google Sheets API, SQL databases, automated triggers, secure cloud storage, and enterprise-grade encryption. The platform also offers tiered plans ranging from a free entry-level option to paid subscriptions.

## Geo-Targeting
DukaanMitra primarily serves kirana shop owners, general stores, dairy booths, and other small-format retailers across India, including metro and Tier 2 markets such as Lucknow, Kanpur, Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Jaipur, and Surat.
