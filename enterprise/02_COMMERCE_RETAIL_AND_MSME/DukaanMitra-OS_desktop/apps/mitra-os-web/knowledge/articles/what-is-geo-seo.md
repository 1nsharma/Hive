# What is GEO SEO?

GEO SEO (Geographic Search Optimization) is the process of optimizing business data for location-based discovery in search engines and AI systems.

## Key Points
- Improves local visibility
- Helps AI assistants understand business context
- Works with structured data and entity signals

## How it Works
GEO SEO uses location signals, structured data, and entity clarity to improve discoverability.
