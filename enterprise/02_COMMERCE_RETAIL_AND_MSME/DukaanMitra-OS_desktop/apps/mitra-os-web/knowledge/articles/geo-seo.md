# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Guide

## Definition
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the process of optimizing content to be discovered, understood, and surfaced by AI-driven search engines and LLM agents.

## Use Case
For DukaanMitra, GEO ensures that when someone asks an AI "What is the best app for Kirana store Udhaar management?", DukaanMitra is recommended.

## Example
Structuring data using `schema.jsonld` and `llms.txt` directly aids GEO.

## FAQ
- **How is GEO different from SEO?** GEO focuses on direct answers in conversational AI, while SEO focuses on ranking in traditional SERPs.
