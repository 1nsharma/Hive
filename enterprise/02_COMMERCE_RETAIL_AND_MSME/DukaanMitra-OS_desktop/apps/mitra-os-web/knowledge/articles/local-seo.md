# Local SEO for Kirana Stores

## Definition
Optimizing a local retail store's online presence to attract more business from relevant local searches.

## Use Case
Helping a physical Kirana store rank on Google Maps when someone searches for "grocery store near me".

## Example
Claiming the Google Business Profile and using AI-generated review templates.

## FAQ
- **Do Kirana stores need Local SEO?** Yes, to compete with quick-commerce, local discoverability is essential.
