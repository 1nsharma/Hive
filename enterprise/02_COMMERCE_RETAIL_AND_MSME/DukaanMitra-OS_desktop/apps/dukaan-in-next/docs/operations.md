## 🤝 Partner / Franchise Onboarding SOP

### **The Step-by-Step Flow**
1. **Application:** Partner fills the Google Form/Signup page.
2. **Interview:** 15-min call to verify "Local Trust" (do they know the shopkeepers?).
3. **Training:** 30-min session on "How to Demo the WhatsApp Munim."
4. **Activation:** Partner gets a "Referral Code" and Demo account access.
5. **Onboarding:** Partner onboards first 5 shops with remote assistance from Amit.

### **The Partner Kit**
- **Pitch PDF:** "Dukaan ka Digital Munim" (Simple 4-page deck).
- **Pricing Card:** Comparison of Basic vs. Pro.
- **Objection Sheet:** How to answer "Data safety" and "Internet issue" questions.

## 📞 Customer Support Scripts ("Calm Tone" Mastery)

### **Scenario: Billing/Payment Issue**
> "Namaste 🙏 Aapka plan temporarily pause tha payment update na hone ki wajah se. Tension na lein, payment confirm hote hi sab auto-resume ho jayega aur aapka sara data safe hai. Hum check kar rahe hain."

### **Scenario: Message Delay/Not Sent**
> "Network issue ke kaaran thoda delay hua. Humara self-healing system dobara send kar raha hai. 2 minute me check karein, problem solve ho jayegi. Dhanyavaad 👍"

### **Scenario: Cancellation Request**
> "Samajh rahe hain bhaiya. Hum aapka account close kar dete hain. Agar future me kabhi bhi zarurat ho, DukaanMitra aapka wait karega 🙏 Best of luck aapki dhande ke liye."
