# 📞 B2B Cold Calling Script: DukaanMitra-OS
> **Target Audience:** Kirana Owners, Supermarket Proprietors, Wholesale Mandi Traders, Multi-Store Retail Chains  
> **Key Decision Maker:** Owner / Proprietor / Managing Partner / Munimji Head  
> **Languages:** Colloquial Hinglish (Primary) & Professional Indian English (Secondary)

---

## 🎯 Section 1: Gatekeeper Navigation (Passing the Counter Boy / Munimji)

### Scenario A: Counter staff answers the phone
- **Closer (Andy):** *"Namaste bhaiya, dukaan par Sethji / [Owner Name] hain kya? Main unke WhatsApp Munim aur counter billing automation verification ke regarding baat kar raha hoon."*
- **Gatekeeper:** *"Sethji abhi counter par busy hain, kya kaam tha?"*
- **Closer Pivot:** *"Bilkul samajh sakta hoon rush time hai. Unhone ek digital catalog aur WhatsApp bill reminder setup ke liye interest dikhaya tha — unka direct mobile number mil sakta hai ya 2 minute me unse connect kara denge? Unka har mahine 4-5 ghanta udhaar recovery me bachna hai."*
- **If Gatekeeper resists:** *"Theek hai bhaiya, aap unhe bata dijiyega Andy Bernard ka call aaya tha DukaanMitra se. Main unke WhatsApp par ek sample digital bill slip bhej raha hoon jisme unki shop ka naam aur 1-tap UPI payment link hai."*

## ⚡ Section 2: The 30-Second Pattern-Interrupt Hook (Direct to Owner)

### The Opening (Hinglish):
> *"Namaste [Owner Name] ji, Andy bol raha hoon DukaanMitra se. Mujhe pata hai aap dukaan par busy honge, isliye bilkul seedha 45 seconds me point pe baat karunga — kya main sahi time par baat kar raha hoon?"*

*(Pause 2 seconds — Let them respond. If they say "Haa bolo" or "Kya hai?")*

> *"[Owner Name] ji, aapke ilaqe ke top retail stores me sham ke time counter par jo rush hota hai, usme do badi pareshaniyan aati hain: pehli, lambi billing queue aur stock tallying me galti; doosri, purane grahako ka udhaar (khata) phasna jise mangne me jhijhak hoti hai.*
> 
> *Humne ek AI WhatsApp Munim banaya hai jisme aapka sales boy sirf WhatsApp par bol kar — jaise 'Ramesh ji ko 2 bori cheeni udhar' — likh ya bol deta hai, aur turant customer ke WhatsApp par digital bill aur 1-tap UPI payment reminder chala jata hai. Zero manual register maintenance.*
> 
> *Aapke counter par abhi billing manual register me hoti hai ya koi software use kar rahe hain?"*

## 🔍 Section 3: Deep Discovery Questions (Uncovering the Pain)

1. **On Credit Leakage & Udhaar Collection:**
   > *"Mahine ke aakhiri hafte me total kitna udhaar market me phasa rehta hai? Aur kya unhe phone karke mangna padta hai ya koi automatic system bhejta hai?"*
2. **On Counter Rush & Staff Dependency:**
   > *"Jab aap counter par nahi hote, toh kya sales boys bina galti ke rate sahi lagate hain? Kitni baar aisa hua ki rate ya discount me loss ho gaya?"*
3. **On ONDC & Online Competition:**
   > *"Blinkit, Zepto aur BigBasket jo 10-minute me delivery de rahe hain, kya unke aane se aapke regular customers ki basket value par koi asar pada hai? Kya aapne ONDC government network par apni dukaan live ki hai?"*
