# 📞 B2B Cold Calling Script: TruckWala 24x7 Fleet & Breakdown SOS
> **Target Audience:** Fleet Owners (10–500 Trucks), 3PL Logistics Providers, Interstate Transport Operators, Cold Chain Fleets  
> **Key Decision Maker:** Managing Director / VP Logistics / Head of Fleet Operations / Transport Contractor  
> **Languages:** Professional Hinglish & English

---

## 🎯 Section 1: Gatekeeper Navigation (Dispatch Office & Traffic Managers)

### Scenario: Fleet Dispatcher or Traffic Coordinator answers
- **Closer (Andy):** *"Namaste, main Andy bol raha hoon TruckWala 24x7 Corporate Operations se. Mujhe aapke Fleet Head / Managing Partner [Owner Name] ji se Highway Emergency SOS coverage aur national breakdown tie-up ke silsile me 2 minute baat karni hai."*
- **Gatekeeper:** *"Sahab abhi loading dock par busy hain. Kya baat thi?"*
- **Closer Pivot:** *"Main bilkul samajhta hoon, transport me loading schedule kitna critical hota hai. Asal me hum Golden Quadrilateral (NH-48, NH-44, NH-19) par 30-minute roadside mechanic dispatch network provide karte hain, taaki highway breakdown par driver ko 10-12 ghante khada na rehna pade.*
- *Kya unka direct mobile number mil sakta hai ya sham ko 5 baje unhe call back karoon? Hum aapke 50 trucks ko free SOS backup de rahe hain."*
- **If Gatekeeper asks to email:** *"Bilkul, par fleet breakdown me ek ghanta late hone se per truck ₹5,000 ka idle loss hota hai. Unhe sirf boliyega TruckWala ke Andy ka call tha, unke Golden Quadrilateral routes par live mechanics ready hain."*

## ⚡ Section 2: The 30-Second Pattern-Interrupt Hook (Direct to Fleet Owner)

### The Opening (Hinglish):
> *"Namaste [Fleet Owner Name] ji, Andy Bernard bol raha hoon TruckWala 24x7 Highway Network se. Mujhe pata hai aap transport operations me boht busy rehte hain, isliye seedha 45 seconds me baat khatam karunga — kya main sahi time par connect hua hoon?"*

*(Pause 2 seconds)*

> *"[Fleet Owner Name] ji, aapke interstate routes jaise Delhi-Mumbai (NH-48) ya Delhi-Kolkata (NH-19) par jab aadhi raat ko truck ka clutch plate, brake booster ya tire burst hota hai, toh do sabse badi dikkat aati hain: pehla, local highway mechanics double-triple overcharge karte hain aur 6-8 ghante aane me lagate hain; doosra, driver akela highway par vulnerable ho jata hai aur customer delivery SLA fail ho jati hai.*
> 
> *TruckWala 24x7 ne 14,000+ verified highway mechanics aur mobile repair vans ka pan-India SOS network tayyar kiya hai, jo kisi bhi highway breakdown location par 30 minutes ke andar physical assistance pahunchata hai — transparent rate card ke sath.*
> 
> *Aapke fleet me abhi highway breakdown handling kaise manage hoti hai — local dhabon ke mechanics par ya koi central backup hai?"*

## 🔍 Section 3: Deep Discovery Questions (Uncovering the Financial Bleed)

1. **On Highway Downtime & Per-Trip Loss:**
   > *"Pichhle mahine highway breakdowns ki wajah se kitne trucks delayed hue? Ek delayed consignment par client kitna penalty ya detention claim karta hai?"*
2. **On Spare Parts & Mechanic Overcharging:**
   > *"Jab driver anjaan highway par kisi local mechanic se repair karwata hai, toh cash bills par kitna dispute hota hai? Kya aapko 100% genuine parts aur verified invoice milte hain?"*
3. **On Telematics & Fleet Visibility:**
   > *"Kya aapke har truck me live GPS aur OBD telematics laga hai jo engine health, coolant temperature aur battery voltage ka real-time alert aapke mobile dashboard par deta hai?"*
