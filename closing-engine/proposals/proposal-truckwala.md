## 1. Executive Summary
Interstate road transport in India accounts for over 70% of domestic freight movement, yet unexpected vehicle breakdowns on National Highways (NH-48, NH-44, NH-19) continue to inflict severe operational delays, driver vulnerability, and unauthorized roadside repair expenses. **TruckWala 24x7** operates a nationwide, technology-driven rapid emergency breakdown response ecosystem.

This proposal establishes the operational terms, service level guarantees, standardized corporate rate schedules, and commercial retainer structure for onboarding **{{companyName}}**'s commercial fleet of **{{fleetSize}}** vehicles.

## 3. Network Infrastructure & Response Capabilities
1. **14,000+ Verified Highway Service Points:** Comprehensive coverage across all Golden Quadrilateral highways, National Expressways (NE-4), and major freight bypasses.
2. **24x7 Centralized Emergency Command Center:** Dedicated logistics dispatch officers managing toll-free helpline, multilingual driver triage (Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil), and rapid dispatch.
3. **OBD Telematics & Remote Diagnostics (`analytics.ts`):** Real-time monitoring of vehicle engine parameters (coolant levels, oil pressure, diagnostic trouble codes) enabling predictive roadside intervention before cataclysmic mechanical failure.
4. **Digital Proof-of-Repair & Maker-Checker Flow:** Every roadside job requires high-resolution photographic proof of the damaged component and replacement part before vehicle clearance is granted.
