## 1. Executive Summary
Commercial and industrial (C&I) power tariffs in India remain among the highest in Asia, averaging ₹9.80 to ₹11.50 per kWh, representing 15% to 30% of total plant operating expenditure. **SolarHub** provides turnkey, high-efficiency rooftop solar microgrids coupled with **SolarSync** peer-to-peer (P2P) IoT energy wallet integration.

Under our **Zero-Capex RESCO / PPA Model**, SolarHub finances, installs, insures, and maintains the entire solar installation on **{{companyName}}**'s facility, delivering guaranteed clean electricity at a fixed tariff of **₹4.50 / kWh** — generating instant bottom-line savings without touching corporate balance sheet reserves.

## 3. Commercial Models: Zero-Capex PPA vs. Direct Buyout
*Financial modeling audited by Kevin (`kevin-analytics`).*

### Model A: Zero-Capex RESCO / PPA (Recommended)
- **Client Capital Expenditure (Capex):** **₹0 (Zero Investment)**.
- **Solar Energy Tariff:** Flat **₹4.50 / kWh** (Escalation fixed at 1.5% annually).
- **Current DISCOM Grid Tariff:** ₹10.20 / kWh (Historical escalation ~4.5% annually).
- **Contract Duration:** 20 Years (Ownership transfers to {{companyName}} at ₹1 terminal value).
- **Operations & Maintenance:** 100% borne by SolarHub (includes cleaning, insurance, and inverter replacements).

### Model B: Direct Capex Buyout (High Equity Return)
- **Turnkey EPC Cost:** ₹38,500 per kWp installed.
- **Accelerated Depreciation:** 40% tax shield under Income Tax Act Section 32 in Year 1.
- **Project IRR:** 28.4% with simple financial payback achieved in **3.2 years**.

## 5. SolarSync P2P Energy Wallet & Weekend Surplus Monetization
During weekend plant shutdowns or festival holidays, facility solar generation continues at peak irradiance:
1. **Automated DISCOM Grid Banking:** Surplus energy is channeled into the state grid under net-metering banking regulations.
2. **Virtual Wheeling / P2P Credit Transfer:** SolarSync energy wallet enables {{companyName}} to credit surplus green units to sister manufacturing units, regional branch offices, or employee electric vehicle charging portals.
