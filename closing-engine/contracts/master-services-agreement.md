### RECITALS
**WHEREAS**, the Service Provider develops, operates, and licenses enterprise AI software solutions, cloud microservices, and operational network engines across retail, logistics, cleantech, and cryptographic governance (including DukaanMitra-OS, TruckWala 24x7, SolarHub, and ProofLedger AI);  
**AND WHEREAS**, the Client desires to engage the Service Provider to provide enterprise software subscriptions, emergency support networks, clean energy microgrids, or cryptographic audit services as specifically enumerated in one or more executed Statements of Work ("SOW");  
**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, and conditions herein contained, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

---

### ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION
1.1 **"Applicable Law"** means any statute, ordinance, rule, regulation, notification, directive, or order issued by any governmental, statutory, or regulatory authority in India, including the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), and Data Protection Board of India.  
1.2 **"Client Data"** means all proprietary transactional records, inventory entries, employee credentials, vehicle telemetry, customer information, and document images uploaded, processed, or generated by the Client through the Services.  
1.3 **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public technical, commercial, financial, operational, or legal information disclosed by one Party to the other Party, whether marked confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential.  
1.4 **"DPDP Act"** means the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and all rules, guidelines, and circulars framed thereunder.  
1.5 **"Intellectual Property Rights" (IPR)** means all patents, copyrights, source code, neural algorithms, trademarks, trade secrets, database rights, and proprietary know-how owned by the Service Provider.  
1.6 **"Statement of Work" (SOW)** means a mutually executed document governed by this Agreement specifying the project scope, deliverable milestones, pricing, and service level agreements (SLAs).

---

### ARTICLE 2: SCOPE OF SERVICES & STATEMENTS OF WORK
2.1 **Provision of Services:** The Service Provider shall deliver the services, software access, and operational dispatch capabilities specified in each applicable SOW with professional diligence and skill.  
2.2 **Execution of SOWs:** Each SOW shall be sequentially numbered, shall incorporate the terms of this MSA by reference, and shall be executed by authorized representatives of both Parties. In the event of a direct conflict between the terms of this MSA and an SOW, the specific terms of the SOW shall prevail solely with respect to that project scope.

---

### ARTICLE 3: COMMERCIAL TERMS, INVOICING & TAXES
3.1 **Fees and Charges:** The Client shall pay the Service Provider the commercial subscription, retainer, energy tariff, or per-incident fees set forth in the applicable SOW.  
3.2 **Invoicing Schedule:** Invoices shall be issued monthly or milestone-based as indicated in the SOW. Payment shall be due within **fifteen (15) calendar days** from the invoice date.  
3.3 **Applicable Taxes:** All commercial fees are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST). The Service Provider shall issue a valid GST tax invoice indicating the appropriate HSN/SAC codes, CGST, SGST, or IGST components.  
3.4 **TDS Compliance:** Any Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under the Income Tax Act, 1961, shall be deducted at the applicable statutory rate, and the Client shall furnish the requisite Form 16A TDS certificate to the Service Provider within statutory timelines.

---

### ARTICLE 4: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS
4.1 **Service Provider Ownership:** The Service Provider retains sole and exclusive ownership of all right, title, and interest in and to DukaanMitra-OS, TruckWala 24x7, SolarHub, ProofLedger AI, including all underlying source code, algorithms, models, system architectures, documentation, and enhancements.  
4.2 **Client Data Ownership:** The Client retains sole ownership of all Client Data. The Service Provider is granted a limited, non-exclusive license to process Client Data solely to the extent necessary to deliver the Services.  
4.3 **No Reverse Engineering:** The Client shall not decompile, reverse engineer, disassemble, or attempt to derive the underlying source code of any proprietary software modules provided by the Service Provider.

---

### ARTICLE 5: DATA PROTECTION & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE (DPDP ACT 2023)
5.1 **Data Fiduciary & Processor Relationship:** For the purposes of the DPDP Act 2023, the Client acts as the **"Data Fiduciary"** and the Service Provider acts as the **"Data Processor"**.  
5.2 **Processor Obligations:** The Service Provider covenants that it shall:  
(a) Process personal digital data strictly in accordance with lawful processing instructions provided by the Client;  
(b) Implement technical and organizational security safeguards to prevent data breaches;  
(c) Maintain zero third-party cloud data leakage by utilizing local-first, on-device (DIP Vision), or encrypted sovereign hosting infrastructure;  
(d) Notify the Client without undue delay (and in no event later than 24 hours) upon becoming aware of any confirmed security incident or personal data breach.

---

### ARTICLE 6: CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 **Non-Disclosure Covenant:** Each Party agrees that it shall maintain the Confidential Information of the other Party in strict confidence and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except to its employees, advisors, and contractors who need to know such information and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Article.  
6.2 **Exclusions:** Confidential Information shall not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure; or (c) is required to be disclosed by order of a competent court or regulatory authority (provided reasonable advance notice is given).  
6.3 **Survival:** The obligations of confidentiality under this Article shall survive for a period of **five (5) years** following the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

---

### ARTICLE 7: WARRANTIES & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
7.1 **Service Warranty:** The Service Provider warrants that the Services shall perform substantially in accordance with the specifications and SLAs detailed in the applicable SOW.  
7.2 **Limitation of Liability:** Except in cases of gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality obligations:  
(a) Neither Party shall be liable to the other Party for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages (including loss of profits, revenue, or business interruption);  
(b) The maximum aggregate liability of the Service Provider arising under this Agreement or any SOW shall be strictly capped at the total commercial fees actually paid by the Client to the Service Provider under the applicable SOW in the **six (6) months** immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability.

---

### ARTICLE 8: TERM, SUSPENSION & TERMINATION
8.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for an initial term of **three (3) years**, automatically renewing for successive one (1) year periods unless terminated in writing by either Party.  
8.2 **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:  
(a) Commits a material breach of this Agreement which remains uncured for thirty (30) days following written notice of default;  
(b) Enters into bankruptcy, liquidation, or insolvency proceedings.  
8.3 **Effect of Termination:** Upon termination, the Client shall pay all outstanding fees accrued up to the effective termination date, and the Service Provider shall securely export and deliver all Client Data within fifteen (15) days, subsequently purging all local copies in compliance with data sanitization standards.

---

### ARTICLE 9: DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ARBITRATION
9.1 **Conciliation:** In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, senior executive representatives of both Parties shall meet and endeavor to resolve the dispute amicably within **fifteen (15) business days**.  
9.2 **Arbitration:** If unresolved through conciliation, the dispute shall be finally settled by binding arbitration in accordance with the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996** (as amended). The arbitration tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the Parties. If the Parties fail to agree upon an arbitrator within thirty (30) days, the appointment shall be made in accordance with the Act.  
9.3 **Seat, Venue & Language:** The legal seat and venue of arbitration shall be **New Delhi, India**. The proceedings shall be conducted in the English language.  
9.4 **Governing Law & Jurisdiction:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Subject to the arbitration clause, the courts at **New Delhi** shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

---

### ARTICLE 10: MISCELLANEOUS
10.1 **Force Majeure:** Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure in performing its obligations due to events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, flood, earthquake, war, government lockdowns, or major telecommunication grid failures.  
10.2 **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be severed and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.  
10.3 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with all executed SOWs and schedules, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior proposals, negotiations, and discussions.
